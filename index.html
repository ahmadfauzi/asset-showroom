How the showroom works

Try textures on the surfaces of a room and see how they look in different light.

Switch rooms

There are four rooms: Living room, Bedroom, Bathroom and Garden. Use the room buttons to switch. Each room keeps its own textures, time of day and undo history while this page is open.

Apply a texture

Pick a surface in the list on the left, or click it in the room. Then click a texture in the tray at the bottom. You can also drag a texture straight onto a surface.

Change the light

Use the presets or the slider to change the time of day. The lamps come on after sunset. On a small screen, the slider is in the tray.

Make it snow

In the Garden, press Snow to turn snow on or off. Undo turns it back.

Move around

Drag to turn the room and scroll to zoom.

Share the room

Copy link puts this room in the link, with its textures, time of day and snow. Anyone who opens it sees the same room.
