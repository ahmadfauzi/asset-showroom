How the showroom works

Try textures on the surfaces of a room and see how they look in different light.

Apply a texture

Pick a surface in the list on the left, or click it in the room. Then click a texture in the tray at the bottom. You can also drag a texture straight onto a surface.

Change the light

Use the presets or the slider on the right to change the time of day. The lamps come on after sunset.

Move around

Drag to turn the room and scroll to zoom.

Share the room

Copy link puts the room in the link. Anyone who opens it sees the same textures and time of day.
